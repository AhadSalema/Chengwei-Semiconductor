# Chengwei Semiconductor

> **Advanced High-Purity Fluid & Gas Delivery Systems for Next-Generation Manufacturing**

**Shanghai Chengwei Semiconductor Equipment Co., Ltd.** is a specialized integrated supplier of critical process system equipment for advanced manufacturing. Established in **September 2025** with a registered capital of **RMB 10 million**, the company is headquartered in **Songjiang District, Shanghai, China**.

Chengwei Semiconductor focuses on high-purity fluid and gas delivery solutions for industries where **contamination control, precision chemical dosing, process reliability, and operational safety** are critical.

# 🧪 Core Product Portfolio

## 1. Chemical Delivery Equipment

Chengwei provides high-purity liquid chemical delivery systems designed for reliable, automated, and contamination-controlled chemical handling.

### Chemical Delivery Modules

Automatic chemical supply equipment for acids, alkalis, and organic solvents.

**Key features:**

* PP/PVC cabinets for acids and alkalis
* Stainless-steel cabinets for organic solvents
* Dual-drum automatic switching
* PFA piping
* Sub-micron filtration
* Automated chemical supply

### Chemical Charging Boxes

High-capacity automated unloading and filtration systems designed for chemical tanker unloading and chemical distribution.

### High-Precision Mixing & Dilution Systems

Advanced chemical blending systems capable of achieving dilution accuracy of:

> **±0.001%**

Designed for applications requiring extremely precise chemical concentration control.

### Slurry Delivery Systems

Specialized **24/7 continuous-circulation systems** for Chemical Mechanical Planarization (CMP) slurry delivery.

**Key technologies include:**

* Magnetic levitation pumps
* High-precision electronic weighing scales
* Continuous circulation
* Automated slurry delivery
* High-precision process control

### Valve Manifold Boxes & Panels

Safe, high-purity multi-channel chemical distribution systems designed for controlled chemical routing and process equipment connections.

## Quality & Testing Standards

| Test                      | Requirement                      |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| **Pressure Holding Test** | Pressure drop ≤ 1% over 24 hours |
| **Helium Leak Test**      | ≤ 1.0 × 10⁻⁹ mbar·L/s            |
| **Particle Count**        | ≤ 1.0 particle at 0.1 μm         |
| **Moisture Increment**    | ≤ 1 ppb                          |
| **Oxygen Increment**      | ≤ 1 ppb                          |

Every system undergoes rigorous testing before delivery to ensure compliance with high-purity process requirements.
